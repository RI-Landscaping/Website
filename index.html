How a job runs

What actually happens.

  1. 01

    Reach out

    Text a few photos and the rough scope, or give us a call. For smaller, clear-cut jobs, that's often enough for a same-day quote.

  2. 02

    Site walk (larger jobs)

    We walk the property together, then follow up with your quote by text.

  3. 03

    Design talk (design jobs)

    For garden bed design or planting layout, we go back and forth over text refining the plan before locking in a price.

  4. 04

    Job day

    The work gets done — installed, planted, or cleared — in one visit where possible, with no debris left behind.
